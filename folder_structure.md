        ├── main.c
        ├── README.md

Example of an excellent exercise folder:

exercises/
└── strings/
    └── ex02_strcpy/
        ├── main.c
        ├── notes.md
        ├── build.sh
        └── README.md

main.c → exercise
notes.md → what you learned
README.md → objective
build.sh → compile command
